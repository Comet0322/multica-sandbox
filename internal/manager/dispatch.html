Share of the live pods

Each workspace's pods against the lending policy's target, the furthest short first.

running holding target guarantee

The target splits the live pods for the work there is now: each workspace's guarantee first, the rest by weight, never more than its pods in use plus its queued tasks. A workspace under its target with tasks queued is waiting for a pod; one under its target with nothing queued only has its guarantee kept. Running tasks are never stopped to rebalance, so pods follow the target as bindings end. The page reads /v1/dispatch every 10 seconds.
