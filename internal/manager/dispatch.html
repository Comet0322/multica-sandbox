When Pod Phase Kind What happened, and what to do

Fair share is how many pods a workspace should have for the work there is now: its guarantee first, then the remaining pods split by weight, never more than its running pods plus its waiting tasks. A guarantee is kept by count: other workspaces may not take the pods it keeps free, whichever pods those are. A workspace below its share with tasks waiting gets the next pod. Running tasks are never stopped to rebalance; when the pod budget is full, idle pods are deleted first, then held pool runtime pods, then held clean pods. The page reads /v1/dispatch every 10 seconds.
